# AI capex: second-half 2026 check

## Summary

Our H2 2026 check shows no sign of an AI capex slowdown: the top four hyperscalers are on track for about USD 520bn of 2026 capex and early 2027 budgets imply roughly 30% growth. Power and packaging, not demand, are the constraints.

## AI capex durability

Our check of hyperscaler commentary suggests AI capital spending will keep growing next year, though at a slower pace than this year.

Spending is broadening from the largest cloud providers to sovereign projects and enterprises, which supports durability.

## Our preferences

We favour suppliers with exposure to both compute and networking, since networking spend is growing faster as clusters scale.

## Risks

A financing pullback by neoclouds; inference efficiency gains reducing compute needs.
