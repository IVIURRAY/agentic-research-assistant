# TSMC: AI accelerators drive ~25% 2026 growth guide

## Summary

TSMC guided 2026 USD revenue growth of close to 25%, with AI accelerator revenue expected to roughly double. 2026 capex guidance is USD 52-56bn. Buy, price target USD 260.

## Thesis

Leading-edge share near 100% for AI accelerators and pricing power at N3 and N2 support margins above 56% despite overseas fab dilution.

## Growth drivers

AI accelerators are the main driver of this year's growth, through both leading-edge wafers and advanced packaging. Smartphone and PC demand is stable but no longer the swing factor in the outlook.

Pricing is a second lever. TSMC has raised prices on its most advanced nodes, and customers have accepted the increases because there is no credible alternative at comparable yields.

## Capacity plans

Capital spending stays elevated to keep pace with AI demand, with a growing share going to advanced packaging and overseas fabs. We think the overseas expansion dilutes margins slightly but reduces geopolitical risk for customers.

## Risks

Geopolitics across the Taiwan Strait; tariff risk on US-bound chips.
