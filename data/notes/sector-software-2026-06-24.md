# Software: Seat-based pricing under AI pressure

## Summary

AI agents threaten seat-based pricing as headcount stops tracking usage. Vendors with consumption or outcome-based pricing (hyperscalers, data platforms) are better placed than per-seat application vendors. We expect a shift to hybrid seat-plus-consumption models through 2027.

## Seat-based pricing

AI tools threaten seat-based pricing models, since fewer human users may be needed for the same work. Software vendors are experimenting with consumption-based pricing.

Vendors with strong data platforms are better positioned to capture AI value through new products.

## Our preferences

We prefer companies that can monetise AI through consumption models rather than relying on seat growth.
