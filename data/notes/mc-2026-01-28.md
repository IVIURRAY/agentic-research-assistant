# LVMH: Weak China keeps Fashion & Leather negative

## Summary

Fashion & Leather organic sales fell 3% in Q4 2025 on weak Chinese demand. Hold, EUR 620 price target.

## China

Chinese consumer demand for luxury goods remains weak, both at home and through travel spending abroad. Fashion and leather goods, LVMH's largest division, is most exposed.

Other divisions, such as perfumes and cosmetics, are holding up better but cannot offset the weakness in fashion and leather goods.

## Our view

We stay at Hold until there are signs of stabilisation in Chinese demand.
