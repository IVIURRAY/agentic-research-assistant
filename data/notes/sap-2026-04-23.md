# SAP: Downgrade to Hold as backlog growth decelerates

## Summary

We downgrade SAP to Hold from Buy and cut our price target to EUR 250 from EUR 300. Current cloud backlog growth decelerated to 22% from 27%, and several US public sector deals slipped out of the quarter.

## Why now

Our Buy case relied on backlog growth staying above 25% through 2026. With that broken, the valuation premium to software peers looks hard to defend.

## Deal timing

Several public sector deals in the US slipped out of the quarter, which management attributed to procurement delays rather than lost business. We accept that explanation but think it signals a longer sales cycle.

Private sector demand in Europe remained solid, so the weakness is concentrated rather than broad-based.

## Valuation premium

SAP trades at a premium to software peers that assumed backlog growth would stay elevated. With that assumption under pressure, we see limited upside from here.

## Risks

Upside: re-acceleration from Business Data Cloud.
