# NVIDIA: 74% gross margin, China excluded from guide

## Summary

Gross margin was 74.1%. Guidance assumes zero China data center revenue. We raise our price target to USD 250; Buy.

## Details

Networking revenue grew faster than compute for the second quarter in a row as customers build larger clusters.

## Margins and mix

Gross margin held at a very high level despite the ramp of complex rack-scale systems, which carry more third-party content. Pricing power remains strong because supply is still the binding constraint for most customers.

Operating expenses are rising quickly as the company invests in software and networking, but revenue growth continues to outpace cost growth.

## China

Management now excludes China from guidance entirely. We see that as a sensible simplification: any approved shipments become upside rather than a source of uncertainty in the forecast.

## Risks

Power availability limiting data center build-outs.
