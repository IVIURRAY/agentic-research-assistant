# TSMC: CoWoS capacity to 110k wafers/month; PT to USD 300

## Summary

We now expect TSMC's CoWoS advanced packaging capacity to reach about 110k wafers per month by end-2026, up from roughly 75k at end-2025. We raise our price target to USD 300 and keep Buy.

## Details

Advanced packaging is the binding constraint on AI accelerator supply; the expansion supports our view that AI revenue doubles again in 2027.

## Advanced packaging

Advanced packaging has become a bottleneck for AI accelerator supply, so capacity additions here matter as much as wafer capacity. TSMC is adding capacity faster than we expected and is partnering with outside assembly firms for overflow work.

We expect packaging to remain tight into next year, which supports pricing and gives TSMC unusual visibility on demand from its largest AI customers.

## Estimate changes

We raise our revenue forecasts for next year and nudge up our long-term margin assumptions, which drives the higher price target.

Packaging yield on larger interposers; customer concentration.
