# ASML: Upgrade to Buy as orders inflect and High-NA broadens

## Summary

We upgrade ASML to Buy from Hold and raise our price target to EUR 1,050 from EUR 880. Q1 2026 net bookings were EUR 7.1bn against consensus of EUR 5.4bn, and the order book now includes two High-NA EXE:5200 systems from a memory customer.

## What changed

Our January caution rested on China normalisation. China fell to 29% of system sales in Q1, faster than we expected, yet total revenue held up because EUV demand from leading-edge logic and DRAM filled the gap. High-NA now has more than one customer, which was our key condition for turning positive.

## Valuation

EUR 1,050 is 32x 2027E EPS of EUR 32.8 (raised from EUR 29.3).

## Order book quality

The mix of the order book matters as much as its size. Memory customers have returned to EUV for advanced DRAM, which broadens the customer base and makes the backlog less dependent on a single foundry's capex plans.

We also note shorter lead times on immersion tools, which suggests the China-driven congestion of last year has cleared and that the remaining backlog is weighted towards higher-value systems.

## Estimate changes

We raise our revenue and margin assumptions for next year to reflect the stronger order intake, and lift our long-term High-NA unit forecasts modestly.

Our gross margin assumptions remain below management's aspiration because the early High-NA systems carry lower margins until volumes build.

## Risks

Order lumpiness; a pause in DRAM capex; further export restrictions.
