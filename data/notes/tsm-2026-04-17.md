# TSMC: Q1 margin strength; N2 ramp in H2; PT to USD 285

## Summary

Q1 2026 gross margin was 58.6%. N2 enters volume ramp in H2 2026. We raise our price target to USD 285 from USD 260; Buy.

## Details

Management expects Arizona fabs to dilute gross margin by 2-3 points in 2026, more than offset by N3 utilisation and price increases.

## Margin drivers

Gross margin strength came from higher utilisation on leading-edge nodes and favourable pricing. Currency also helped, though we do not treat that as repeatable.

The dilution from overseas fabs was smaller than feared, as early yields at the new sites have improved faster than the company guided.

## Next-node ramp

The next-generation node remains on schedule for a second-half ramp, with the first customers in smartphones followed by AI accelerators. Early design wins look broader than they were for the previous node at the same stage.

Tariffs; overseas cost inflation.
