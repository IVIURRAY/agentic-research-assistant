# Obesity market: price erosion scenarios

## Summary

We model US GLP-1 net prices falling 35-45% by 2030 as volume expands through oral formulations and Medicare coverage. In our base case the obesity market still reaches USD 120bn by 2030, but the incumbents' share falls as competition and generics in ex-US markets arrive.

## Price erosion

Obesity drug prices are falling as competition intensifies and payers push back. Volume growth should offset much of the price decline over time.

Oral formulations and next-generation drugs could support pricing by offering differentiated benefits.

## Scenarios

Our scenarios range from moderate price erosion with strong volume growth to steeper price cuts that compress margins for the leading players.
