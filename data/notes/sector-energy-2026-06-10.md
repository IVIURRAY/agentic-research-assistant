# Oil majors: Buyback sustainability at USD 65 Brent

## Summary

At USD 65 Brent, we estimate Shell can sustain buybacks of about USD 3bn a quarter from free cash flow, while BP cannot fund more than USD 0.75bn without raising gearing. Balance sheet strength is the key differentiator.

## Buyback sustainability

At lower oil prices, majors with strong balance sheets can sustain buybacks, while those with high debt may need to cut.

Integrated gas and trading businesses provide resilience when oil prices are weak.

## Our preferences

We favour majors with low gearing and significant LNG exposure.
