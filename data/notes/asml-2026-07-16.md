# ASML: Q2 beat, 2027 growth reaffirmed; PT to EUR 1,120

## Summary

Q2 2026 gross margin was 53.8%, above guidance of 51-52%, and management reaffirmed that 2027 will be a growth year. We raise our price target to EUR 1,120 and reiterate Buy.

## Details

The EUV backlog stands at EUR 38bn. Installed-base management revenue grew 14% year on year. China was 27% of system sales.

## Valuation

EUR 1,120 is 32x 2027E EPS of EUR 35.0.

## Management commentary

Management struck a confident tone on the growth outlook and stressed that customer conversations have shifted from whether to adopt High-NA to when. That is the change in language we were waiting for at the start of the year.

On China, management described demand as normalising rather than collapsing, with mature-node customers still placing orders for immersion tools at a steadier pace.

## What we are watching

The next catalysts are memory capex budgets for next year and any update on High-NA insertion timing at the leading logic customers. Both feed directly into our unit forecasts.

## Risks

Customer timing of High-NA insertion; currency.
