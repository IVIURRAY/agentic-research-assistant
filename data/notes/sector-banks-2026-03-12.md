# European banks: Rate cuts and capital return

## Summary

European bank NII has proven more resilient to rate cuts than feared due to structural hedges. Capital return yields of 8-10% remain attractive; we prefer banks with excess capital and hedge tailwinds such as HSBC.

## Rates and earnings

Falling rates will pressure net interest income, but structural hedges and deposit repricing should cushion the impact for most European banks.

Fee income from wealth and asset management is becoming more important as rate tailwinds fade.

## Capital return

Most banks hold excess capital, supporting continued buybacks and dividends. We favour banks that combine generous distributions with resilient earnings.
