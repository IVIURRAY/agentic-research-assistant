# ASML: Field check flags High-NA timing at TSMC; stay Buy

## Summary

Our supply-chain checks suggest TSMC will not insert High-NA EUV at the A14 node until 2028, a year later than some investors assume. We see this as a modest risk to 2027 High-NA unit forecasts, not to the thesis, and keep Buy with an unchanged EUR 1,120 price target.

## Implications

Low-NA EUV multi-patterning at A14 is EUV-intensive in its own right, so total EUV layer counts still rise. We trim 2027 High-NA units from 14 to 11.

## Field check detail

Our conversations with equipment engineers and supply-chain contacts point to the leading foundry extending current EUV multi-patterning for longer before switching to High-NA. The reason given is cost per layer rather than any technical problem.

Memory customers appear more willing to move early, which partly offsets the foundry delay. We see this as a timing shift within the High-NA ramp rather than a change to its eventual size.

## Why we stay Buy

Low-NA EUV demand remains strong and the installed base keeps growing, which supports servicing revenue regardless of High-NA timing. The investment case does not depend on any single year of High-NA shipments.

## Risks

If memory customers also delay High-NA, 2027 consensus would need to fall.
